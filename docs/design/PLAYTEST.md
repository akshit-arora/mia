# V1 Playtest Script — 5 children, ages 6-7

One page. Source: `V1_BRIEF.md` (metrics, stop rule). Purpose: prove the child, not the adult, does the thinking. Whoever runs it is a silent observer, not a teacher.

## Setup (10 min before)
- Target phone, landscape, charged, volume up, captions on. Fresh profile per child (clear `mia.cards.v1`; no `?unlock=all`, no `?debug`).
- Device on a table or held by the child; observer sits beside and slightly behind. Phone screen-recorded if the parent consents; otherwise a second adult times and tallies.
- Giraffe props ready, kept hidden until minute 6: three paper cards (leaves on a tall branch / grass on the ground / fish in a bowl), one giraffe picture on its own card. Giraffe is NOT in the game.
- Stopwatch and the metrics sheet below. Parent briefed: no hints, no pointing, no "try this".
- 10 min per child, one child at a time, no siblings watching.

## Script
1. Hand over the phone with the home screen showing. Say only: **"This is a game. Have a go."** Start the clock.
2. After that, say nothing instructional. If asked "what do I do?" reply **"What do you think?"** (count it). If stuck 30 s, the observer may say "keep going" only; any real help = adult intervention (count it).
3. Minute 0-6: free play. Observe, do not talk. Tally each item below as it happens.
4. Minute 6: stop the game gently: **"Let's try something else for a moment."** Show the giraffe card: **"This animal is hungry. It lives somewhere far away. Which of these would you give it?"** Never say what a giraffe eats; never ask what a bear eats. Then ask only **"Why?"** Record the choice and the reason verbatim (neck, tall, "it reaches" = pass).
5. Return to the game until minute 8. At 8:00 say: **"You can stop whenever you like."** Start a 60 s window; record if they keep playing or ask for another go.
6. Close: thank the child; ask "Who came with you on the trail?" (if a buddy was used) and "What did your friend do?"

## What to observe (tick per child)
- First action time (stopwatch from handover).
- Waiting for a glow/hint: does the child pause >10 s? touch only highlighted things?
- Wandering: leaves the Jungle (farm/house door) or loses interest after one run.
- Reaction to behaviours: chases bouncing fruit, laughs at honey bees, notices heavy sugarcane.
- Basket decision: does the child leave a food after 5? Any distress at bumps?
- Buddy: pointing at or naming the buddy; naming what it did.
- Feeding: finds the tap alone; reaction to the stamp.

## Stop rule
If **2 or more** children wait for a glow, or play one run and wander: **stop adding content**. Remove hints, add a real choice. Do not fix it with art. Write the result into `V1_DONE.md` section 5 before any further build work.

## After each child (2 min)
Note verbatim quotes, the exact moment of any hesitation, and what the child said about the buddy. Reset the profile. Do not change the build between children; change only after all five.
